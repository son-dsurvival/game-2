### Background
<!-- evidence-id: character_relationship.character_relationships.player.background -->

Femi D'survival is the player-controlled main character: a 25-year-old Human from Tosun Estate, on the outskirts of Lagos, Nigeria. He is an applied AI engineer, analytical, private, and relentlessly curious about how systems fit together. Over five years he built a home that doubles as a laboratory — rooftop solar, a 10 kWh battery bank, a 24 GB-VRAM workstation, an offline knowledge archive, local automation, and Ava, a personal assistant he integrated rather than invented. He is comfortable alone and prone to overanalyzing, has no combat training, and depends on irreplaceable hardware. His class label at setup is Cognitive Linker (provisional setup label), and he begins at Tier 0. On Day Zero he is at home with Dara Adeyemi.
